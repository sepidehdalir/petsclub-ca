Dog boots are easiest to compare after you know which measurement each model uses. A width-based chart and a length-based chart can give different answers for the same paw. Start with the exact product instructions, record all four paws, then compare package quantity, care and return conditions.

This guide is for everyday shopping decisions. It is not a tested ranking or a recommendation for treating injured paws. We checked the linked manufacturer and veterinary sources on October 18, 2026. The Pet Club has not tested these products.

## Start with the walk you actually take

Write down the reason you are considering boots: salted sidewalks, snow accumulating around the feet, wet conditions, or a particular outdoor surface. That gives you something specific to ask a retailer about instead of choosing whichever listing says “all terrain.”

The [Canadian Veterinary Medical Association's cold-weather guidance](https://www.canadianveterinarians.net/related-resources/cold-weather-and-pets) explains that road salt can irritate paws and that washing feet and considering protective booties can reduce risk. Boots are one precaution. They do not establish a safe outdoor temperature or remove the need to watch for discomfort.

If there is an injured paw, persistent licking or a change in movement, ask your veterinary team about suitable protection before buying. An ordinary walking boot is not automatically appropriate over a bandage.

For the wider seasonal routine, see our [winter dog-care guide](/guides/winter-dog-care-in-canada).

## Read the measurement method before taking the measurement

Two real examples show why a universal sizing shortcut is unhelpful:

- [Ruffwear's current Polar Trex chart](https://ruffwear.ca/products/polar-trex-winter-dog-boots) uses the widest part of the paw while it bears weight. It says to measure all paws and size down between sizes.
- [Muttluks' Original Fleece-Lined chart](https://muttluks.ca/pages/dog-boots-size-chart) uses the distance from the longest toenail to a mark below the back of the leg. Its instructions include nails and exclude fur. Breed examples are approximate, not measurements.

These instructions belong to those particular products. Do not add an allowance from a third-party article or apply one brand's between-sizes rule to another. If a chart is ambiguous, send the manufacturer the measurements it requests and ask which size it recommends. A guessed size becomes more expensive if the item cannot be returned after an outdoor trial.

## Compare two designs without assuming a winner

These are manufacturer-listed examples available through Canadian storefronts, not a complete market survey. A listing does not confirm stock in your size or delivery to your address. The links below are ordinary, non-affiliate links.

### Ruffwear Polar Trex: a two-boot package with winter-specific details

The current [Canadian Polar Trex product page](https://ruffwear.ca/products/polar-trex-winter-dog-boots) lists pairs of boots, a waterproof and windproof softshell upper, a pullover gaiter, and a winter outsole with glass-fibre lugs. It specifies outdoor use only. Care is hand washing with mild detergent and air drying.

The practical questions are whether separate front and rear sizes suit your dog, whether you can manage the gaiter, and where the manufacturer permits an initial fitting trial. Do not follow a generic “walk around indoors first” instruction for an outdoor-only sole. Confirm which version the retailer is selling; an older product photograph or review may describe different closures or materials.

## Compare the cost of a usable set

A pair and a four-boot pack are not directly comparable shelf prices. For each candidate, write down the number of packages and sizes needed to equip the paws you intend to cover. Then add the actual delivery charge, taxes shown at checkout, and any necessary care supplies.

Use this shortlist:

- Exact model, version and size combination
- Boots included per package and packages required
- Delivered total in Canadian dollars
- Whether a fitting trial is permitted, and where
- Return or exchange deadline and condition requirements
- Return postage or other stated charges
- Cleaning directions and any additional maintenance
- Whether replacement singles or pairs are sold, if that matters to you

Do not assume “Canadian retailer” means every postal code qualifies for the same shipping offer. Ask the seller about unclear terms before exposing a new pair to outdoor dirt. Keep the packaging and receipt while you resolve the fit.

## Make the first outing a fitting check

Read the purchased model's fastening instructions and use only a trial location permitted for that sole. Keep the first attempt manageable, supervise the dog and stop if the boots appear uncomfortable, rotate or slip. Do not keep tightening an unsuitable boot to make the purchase work.

Both [Ruffwear](https://ruffwear.ca/products/polar-trex-winter-dog-boots) and [Muttluks](https://muttluks.ca/products/original-fleece-lined-muttluks) call for checking adjustment during use, including around the first 15 minutes. That is not a reason to wait if something looks wrong earlier. Remove the boots after use and inspect the paws and equipment before the next outing.

A dog that will not use a particular boot comfortably may need a different fit or a different plan. Do not assume resistance is disobedience or that every failure can be solved by more practice. Get fitting help from the manufacturer; bring pain or altered movement to the veterinary team.

## A purchase decision you can explain

Choose the option for which you can answer three questions: “Why this design for this walk?”, “How did I choose these sizes?”, and “What happens if the fit is wrong?” Leave a candidate off the shortlist when a necessary answer is missing.
