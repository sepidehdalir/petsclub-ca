The right carrier has to fit your cat, close securely and suit the journey. A carrier for occasional local appointments may be a different purchase from one for an airline cabin.

Start with those requirements before comparing colours or a “best carrier” list. This guide uses manufacturer specifications, Canadian retail listings and published animal-welfare guidance. We have not tested these products on cats or assessed their crash performance.

## The short version

- Check your cat's measurements and the exact model's intended weight limit.
- Look at usable space, entry points, closures and cleaning access.
- Confirm the transport operator's current requirements before buying for a flight.
- Check the Canadian seller's selected size, delivery cost and return conditions.

## Begin with the journey

For a carrier mainly used for local appointments, look closely at sturdy construction and how the top comes off. A removable upper section gives access without requiring everything to come through a small front door. The [Edmonton Humane Society's selection guide](https://www.edmontonhumanesociety.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-choosing-cat-carrier-EHS-Resources.pdf) recommends secure assembly, generous access and enough space for the cat's normal positions.

For a flight, begin with the operating airline. As checked on 17 October 2026, [Air Canada requires flexible, soft-sided carriers for pets travelling in the cabin](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/plan/special-assistance/pets.html). Its requirements also cover the animal's fit, under-seat space and booking restrictions. Check the actual aircraft and itinerary with the airline; another operator may have different rules.

A retailer's “airline approved” label does not settle those checks for a particular trip. A carrier can suit your cat at home and still fail a flight's requirements.

## Check size in two ways

First, measure your cat according to the manufacturer's instructions and compare those measurements with the exact model and size. Then check the intended maximum animal weight. A weight rating alone cannot tell you whether a long or tall cat has enough room.

The Edmonton Humane Society recommends room for a cat to stand, sit, lie down and turn around comfortably. Use that as a practical fit check alongside the product instructions. Ask whether the listed dimensions describe the outside of the carrier or the usable space inside, especially when the carrier has a thick liner or a curved shell. [Read the society's guide](https://www.edmontonhumanesociety.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-choosing-cat-carrier-EHS-Resources.pdf).

Do not transfer “small” or “medium” from one brand to another. Record centimetres and kilograms where available, and keep the selected product's specification sheet with your shortlist.

## Hard and soft carriers: what to compare

### Hard-sided carriers

Check whether the top lifts off, how the halves fasten together and whether you can reach the inside for cleaning. Look at the door latch as closely as the carry handle. You will need to assemble and close the carrier correctly every time it is used.

[Cat Friendly Homes, created by the Feline Veterinary Medical Association](https://catfriendly.com/be-a-cat-friendly-caregiver/cat-carriers/), highlights access through the top and front, a removable upper section and a cleanable, leak-resistant base as useful buying considerations.

### Soft-sided carriers

Check the base support, mesh panels, zipper closures and the size of the openings. Find out which parts can be washed and whether the liner can be removed. Confirm how the bag should be carried and stored using the model's instructions.

Soft construction may be required for a particular airline cabin, but that does not establish the fit or suitability of every soft carrier. Choose the exact size only after checking both your cat and the operator's requirements.

## Questions to ask before paying

Make a short comparison for each model:

1. Does the intended size and weight range fit this cat?
2. Can I operate every latch or zipper reliably?
3. Can I clean the base, liner and any seams that may collect debris?
4. Are replacement liners or essential parts available?
5. What instructions apply to carrying it and placing it in a vehicle?
6. If flying, has the airline confirmed the carrier requirements for my itinerary?
7. Can I return it after checking fit, and must tags or packaging remain attached?
8. What is the total in Canadian dollars, including delivery, tax and any return postage?

For vehicle use, check the exact product's instructions and evidence. A shoulder strap, belt loop or travel label by itself does not establish crash-test performance. Do not assume the same securing method applies to every carrier.

## Have it ready before the trip

Read the assembly and closure instructions at home. Inspect the carrier and keep essential parts together. Cat Friendly Homes suggests leaving the carrier in a familiar area with bedding so it can become part of the cat's surroundings. Its [carrier guidance](https://catfriendly.com/be-a-cat-friendly-caregiver/cat-carriers/) explains that preparation in more detail.

If your cat struggles with travel or needs individual handling advice, speak with your veterinary team. This buying guide does not assess a cat's fitness to travel or recommend treatment.
