## Choose the right format first

For a puppy learning everyday skills, ask about age requirements, vaccination policies, class size and how much individual help is included. For a fearful or reactive dog, ask whether private assessment is more appropriate than a busy group class. A trainer's general services do not mean every course suits every dog.

The [American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior's humane-training statement](https://avsab.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/AVSAB-Humane-Dog-Training-Position-Statement-2021.pdf) supports reward-based training. Ask exactly what happens when a dog struggles, which equipment is used and whether you can observe a class. Website descriptions of methods are the businesses' own statements, not an independent practice audit. Sudden behaviour changes or signs of pain warrant a veterinary discussion; a directory cannot diagnose a behavioural or medical problem.

## Questions to ask before paying

<Checklist title="Compare the written offer, not just the stars">

- Who will deliver the sessions, and which qualifications can you independently verify?
- What methods and equipment are used? What happens if your dog becomes frightened or overwhelmed?
- Is the session private, in-home, online or a group, and is your dog eligible?
- What does the total include: session length, number of visits, travel, follow-up and materials?
- What are the cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms?
- How will progress be assessed without a guarantee that every behaviour will disappear?

</Checklist>
