## The Pet Club's TikTok creator channel: how to qualify it
- Historical user-provided project context identifies a pet TikTok account with cats/dogs; a prior photo displayed **@petsclub.ca**, and old figures of about 1.3k followers. Those are *not* verified current metrics; do not put audience sizes, engagement or the handle into a brand pitch until login or current public account check verifies them. Public TikTok profiles may be blocked from automated retrieval.
- [TikTok One official sign-up](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-creators-can-sign-up-for-tiktok-one?lang=en): creator must be 18+, comply with branded-content rules; project participation generally starts at 1,000+ followers plus varying project conditions. If the account meets actual requirements, use Creator Marketplace / TikTok Studio. Do not assert enrollment or eligibility from old screenshots.
- [Canadian Competition Bureau influencer disclosure](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act): clearly disclose money, commission, gifts and discounts *on each post*, not just in bio or hashtags. Commercial reviews require real first-hand experience. UGC, video usage rights, ads whitelisting, content revisions and exclusivity require separate negotiated terms.
- Build an honest micro-creator media kit **from current verified analytics only**: handle; post dates; total followers; median views of the last 10 videos; audience geography; recent engagement; content examples; two or three sample creative concepts (do not claim any historical brand collaborations); website URL. If some fields unavailable say `not verified` and omit public-facing claims.
- Proposed creator offer mix: (a) paid original video UGC, brand can use under negotiated term; (b) creator-channel post with a clearly disclosed material relationship; (c) site-native product decision aid if editorially relevant and source-verified; (d) affiliate commission on actual eligible orders. Negotiate fees **without claiming a rate or view guarantee**, and separate posting from paid-ad usage rights.

### Decision gate for any spend
1. No purchase until owner approves exact amount, publisher, contract and targeted outcome. Confirm taxes, currency, cancellation, whether native URL is indexed, and ad-readership evidence.
2. Confirm audience match: Canada's actual pet owners, provincial mix, monthly Canadian visitors and real average referral clicks per sponsored post, **not DA/DR alone**.
3. Confirm transparent ad disclosure and **`rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow` for all paid destination links** (Google's [link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). Never pay for dofollow or exact-match SEO anchors, expired domains, link networks, mass directory links or automatic placements.
4. Keep campaign parameters and conversion events testable **without collecting personal data unexpectedly**; set campaign success metric as qualified referral clicks, email signups (with consent), or attributed sales only if independently verifiable. Break-even calculations require real revenue per converted visit; it is not known today.
5. Seek *earned* editorial links separately: free useful cat-budget worksheet / local official bylaw directory / verified source guide, editor freely chooses whether to cite it. No guarantee or demanded backlink.
