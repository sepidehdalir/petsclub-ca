# Insurance editorial review — 2026-10-09

Decision: revised educational draft prepared in PR #13, not approved for publication. Main was reconciled into the existing branch; its draft state remains in-review. No ranking, quotes, premiums, affiliate activation or enrollment.

## Findings resolved in the draft

- Removed unsupported claims about cheapest enrollment, most common denial cause, lifetime insurance return and accident cover being universally cheapest.
- Removed universal deductible-before-reimbursement wording. Multiple current sample policies use the opposite order.
- Replaced wellness-is-a-payment-plan wording: product structure can include an insurance endorsement.
- Qualified pre-existing exclusions and possible reconsideration of a resolved condition.
- Made continuing-condition coverage and age-related benefit changes explicit comparison questions.
- Corrected complaint guidance: final position letter, subscriber/mandate eligibility, non-binding GIO recommendations, and limits of FSRA's role.
- Removed the invitation to participate in nonexistent community conversations.
- Updated key takeaways, source links and unresolved verification queue to match the revised body.
